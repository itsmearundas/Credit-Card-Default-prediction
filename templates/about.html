About CreditRisk AI

An advanced machine learning platform designed to revolutionize credit risk assessment through intelligent data analysis and predictive modeling.

🎯 Our Mission

CreditRisk AI was developed to provide financial institutions with a powerful, accurate, and efficient tool for assessing credit card default risk. By leveraging state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms and comprehensive data analysis, we help organizations make informed lending decisions that minimize risk while maximizing opportunity.

Our platform analyzes multiple factors including payment history, credit utilization, demographic information, and behavioral patterns to deliver precise risk assessments in real-time.

🔬 Technology & Methodology

Our prediction model is built using Random Forest classification, a robust ensemble learning method that combines multiple decision trees to achieve superior accuracy and reliability. The model has been trained on extensive historical credit data and validated through rigorous testing procedures.

Age, education level, marital status, and other key profile indicators.

💰 Credit Utilization
